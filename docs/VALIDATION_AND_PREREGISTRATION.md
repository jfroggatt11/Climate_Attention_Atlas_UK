# Validation and pre-registration handover

This is the review worksheet for moving from a fixture release to a research
release. It is intentionally a checklist: passing a software audit does not make
the topic phrases or associations scientifically validated.

## Configuration review

- [ ] Native-speaker review completed for English, Welsh and any additional UK
  language phrases.
- [ ] Phrase precision/recall sample labelled and results recorded by topic.
- [ ] Political-signal precision/recall sample labelled separately.
- [ ] Outlet-to-country attribution audit completed.
- [ ] Article URL retention and republication policy approved.
- [ ] Bluesky DIDs, categories, review status and deletion/update handling approved.

## Denominator and source audits

- [ ] GDELT topic numerator and captured-news denominator use identical dates,
  geography and collection coverage.
- [ ] Provider outage dates remain missing rather than zero.
- [ ] Bluesky panel denominator records panel size, completeness and cursor state.
- [ ] MODIS baseline years, valid-area coverage and monthly timing are checked.
- [ ] GDACS named events and FIRMS hotspots remain separate constructs.
- [ ] Search, price, weather, rail and local-disruption sources have source-specific
  units and revision rules.
